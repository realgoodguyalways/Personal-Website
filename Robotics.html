Robotics

As of writing this, I have 5 years of First robotics experience, I started in 2021. I have spent 4 years on an FTC team, and 2 years on an FRC team. I was the operator all five years I have participated in robotics, and I have been the lead programmer for three of those years.

During my four years on FTC, my team was able to make it to states three of the four years, one of which, when I was the Lead programmer, we went undefeated in our states division, during both our qualifier and playoff matches!

During my first year on FRC, I was the not the lead programmer, but I heavily contributed to the development of my team's code, working alongside my teams lead programmer.

On my second year on FRC, the previous lead programmer graduated, so I rose to the lead programmer position. Along with the help of my father, I led my team in designing and building a modified kitbot which we ended up taking to our first competition, while more experienced members built a better robot.
