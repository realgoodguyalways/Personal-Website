Theremin

The original Theremin Instrument had two antennas, one to change pitch, and one to change volume. These would detect the distance by using the players hand as a ground.


My project, unlike the original Theremin instrument, uses an ultrasonic sensor to detect a distance from the users hand. Based on this distance, it sends a signal over BLE to my phone which interperets that signal and outputs a MIDI note.
